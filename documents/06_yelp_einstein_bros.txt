---

I've been to Einstein Bros a few times while working at Cal State East Bay. The prices are kind of expensive for what you get. A medium hot chocolate is $4.79 plus tax, a bagel with cream cheese is $4.09 plus tax, muffins are $2.99 plus tax. I wish the prices were a bit lower so students could actually afford to come more often. The customer service is decent and the staff are friendly. No seating inside but there is outdoor seating with some shade. Overall a decent campus eatery but prices are high.

I've been here a few times between classes. The food is decent but after multiple bad experiences I can't recommend it. Wait times are ridiculously long even when ordering ahead online. The app says your order is ready but when you arrive you still wait 10–30 extra minutes. Staff seem to ignore customers standing at the counter. After my most recent visit I believe I got food poisoning from a bagel sandwich — severe stomach issues within 6 hours. Between the wait times, inattentive staff, and questionable food handling, it's not worth it.
