Source: CSUEB Food Recommendations Guide (Official)
Topic: Off-Campus Restaurants Near Cal State East Bay (Hayward, CA)

BRONCO BILLY'S PIZZA PALACE
Rating: 3.5 stars
Address: 26775 Hayward Blvd, Hayward, CA 94542 (University Plaza)
Distance from campus: ~1 mile
Hours: Thursday 11am–9pm, Friday and Saturday 11am–10pm
Description: Casual pizza parlor serving large pizzas with creative toppings, plus sandwiches and wings. Frequented by CSUEB students. Service can be inconsistent but portions are good.
Student notes: Good portions. Service varies. Popular with students for its proximity.

DON GASPACHO PALETERIA & SNACKS
Rating: 4.6 stars
Address: 364 W Tennyson Rd, Hayward, CA 94544
Distance from campus: ~2.7 miles
Hours: All days 2:30pm–8:30pm
Description: Mexican-style ice cream, paletas, and frozen treats. Also serves hot food and snacks.
Student notes: Student recommendation. Great menu variety. Friendly staff.

LOS DOS HERMANOS
Rating: 4.2 stars
Address: 28257 Mission Blvd, Hayward, CA 94544
Distance from campus: ~2 miles
Hours: Thursday 10am–10pm, Friday and Saturday 9am–10pm
Description: Local Mexican restaurant. Known for good portions, quick service, and quality food. Local family business.
Student notes: Quick and friendly service. Good portions. Locally owned.

FRANNIE'S RESTAURANT
Rating: 4.1 stars
Address: 219 Harder Rd, Hayward, CA 94544
Distance from campus: ~1.8 miles
Hours: All days 11am–8pm
Description: Local Chinese restaurant with good food at a good price. Notable: they have a fish tank.
Student notes: Student recommendation. Good portions. Good price-to-quality ratio.

LE PARADIS & LA PATISSERIE
Rating: 4.4 stars
Address: 22809 Mission Blvd, Hayward, CA 94541
Distance from campus: ~1.5 miles
Hours: All days 10:30am–8:30pm
Description: Local Vietnamese restaurant serving traditional Vietnamese cuisine and French pastries. Family-owned business.
Student notes: Student favorite. Wide variety of food. Family-owned, welcoming atmosphere.

ACQUA E FARINA
Rating: 4.3 stars
Address: 22622 Main St, Hayward, CA 94541
Distance from campus: ~1.8 miles
Hours: Thursday and Friday 12pm–8pm, Saturday 12pm–9pm
Description: Traditional Italian restaurant with good ambiance and a well-stocked cocktail bar. More upscale than typical student spots. Free bread served.
Student notes: High quality service. Described as a "good date or celebration spot." Free bread.

THE DIRTY BIRD LOUNGE & TAILFEATHER KITCHEN
Rating: 3.7 stars (lounge) / 4.5 stars (kitchen)
Address: 926 B St, Hayward, CA 94541
Distance from campus: ~1.8 miles
Hours: All days 2pm–1:30am
Description: Lounge and restaurant combination with a diverse menu of food and drinks. Good daytime ambiance, fun vibe at night. Indoor and outdoor seating.
Student notes: Great cocktails. Quality bar food. Good for evenings.

SUSIE Q'S PIZZA
Rating: 4.4 stars
Address: 22580 Foothill Blvd, Hayward, CA 94541
Distance from campus: ~1.7 miles
Hours: Thursday and Saturday 11am–11pm, Friday 11am–2am
Description: Charming pizza parlor with more than pizza — specialty pizzas, cheesesteaks, pastas, and desserts. Confirmed Halal.
Student notes: Good service. Confirmed Halal. High quality ingredients.
