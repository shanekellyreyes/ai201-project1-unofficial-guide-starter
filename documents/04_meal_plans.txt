Source: CSUEB University Housing & Residence Life — Meal Plan Fees (Official, 2026–2027)
Topic: Meal Plans at Cal State East Bay

IMPORTANT: Meal plans are REQUIRED for all first-year students living in the residence halls.
Meal plans are also available (but not required) for commuter students.

TWO MEAL PLAN OPTIONS:

BLACK 15 PLAN
- 15 meal swipes per week (usable at Pioneer Kitchen for breakfast, lunch, dinner, or late night)
- $100 Flex Dollars per semester (usable at any Campus Dining location — works like a declining balance)
- 5 Meal Exchange swipes per week (equivalent to one meal swipe, usable at select retail locations)
- 5 Guest meal swipes per semester
- Flex Dollar add-on discount: 10%
Cost: Fall semester $3,102 / Spring semester $3,153

RED 19 PLAN
- 19 meal swipes per week (more swipes, more flexibility)
- 7 Meal Exchange swipes per week (usable at select retail locations)
- 5 Guest meal swipes per semester
- Flex Dollar add-on discount: 15%
Cost: Fall semester $3,396 / Spring semester $3,453

HOW IT WORKS:
- Meal Swipes: Used at the Pioneer Kitchen (Dining Commons) for all-you-care-to-eat meals
- Flex Dollars: Work like a campus debit card, accepted at all Campus Dining locations (Panda Express, Taco Bell, Wild Blue, Einstein Bros, Starbucks, Market, etc.)
- Meal Exchange: One swipe = one meal at select retail locations (not the dining hall)
- Unused meal swipes do NOT roll over week to week. Unused weekly swipes are not refunded.
- Flex Dollars are disbursed each semester. Weekly meal swipes reload every Monday.
- Boost App: You can use Flex Dollars through the Boost app to mobile-order at campus eateries, skipping the line.

FINANCIAL SUPPORT:
- Installment payment plan options available
- Financial Aid eligible
- $40 application fee to secure your housing contract

BENEFITS OF A MEAL PLAN (per university):
- Saves time versus cooking, grocery shopping, and cleaning
- All-you-care-to-eat at the Dining Commons
- Per-meal cost is lower than eating out at local eateries
- Flex Dollars and Meal Exchange add flexibility beyond the dining hall

HOUSING CONTEXT:
The meal plan is bundled with on-campus housing. 2026–2027 housing rates:
- Suites Triple (Black 15 plan): $15,864 total for the year
- Suites Double (Black 15 plan): $18,509 total for the year
- Suites Super Single (Black 15 plan): $21,060 total for the year
(Meal plan cost is included in these totals)
