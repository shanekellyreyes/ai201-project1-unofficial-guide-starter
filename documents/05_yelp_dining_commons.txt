If you're an East Bay student, be thankful you didn't sign up for a meal plan. If you have a friend who invites you to the dining commons... run the other way. It's a trap.

This is a buffet-style setup. The food is not fresh. The place is dirty. The daily dinner entree is generally pretty bad and you'll wait in line for 20 minutes to get it. The DC is only open a few hours a day. Dinner costs $9.50 without a meal plan. The only reason this place stays in business is because students are forced to eat here — there's really nowhere else for them to go.
