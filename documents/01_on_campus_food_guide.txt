Source: CSUEB Food Recommendations Guide (Official)
Topic: On-Campus Dining Options at Cal State East Bay

PIONEER KITCHEN (Dining Commons)
Location: Pioneer Heights residential area
Description: The main dining commons at CSUEB. Offers all-you-care-to-eat meals during the week from morning to evening. Menu includes healthy foods and vegetarian selections, a variety of salads, hot foods, desserts, and a rotating Chef's Special that highlights foods from different cultures. Accepts meal plan swipes. Freshmen living on campus are required to purchase a meal plan. Meal plans are also available to commuter students.

TAQUERIA ANGELICA'S
Location: Old University Union (enter from main walkway, walk to back of building)
Description: Mexican food. Menu includes tacos, burritos, burrito bowls, and other Mexican-American options. Replaced the previous Fry Shack location.
Hours (regular semester): Thursday 10:30am–4pm, Friday 10:30am–2pm, Saturday Closed

WILD BLUE
Location: Old University Union (enter from main walkway, walk to back of building)
Description: Poke bar with a variety of poke bowl options. Also sells packaged sushi rolls at their booth and in the Market convenience store.
Hours (regular semester): Thursday 11am–7pm, Friday 11am–2pm, Saturday Closed

PANDA EXPRESS
Location: New University Union (front of building, off the main walkway)
Description: American Chinese fast food. Standard Panda Express menu.
Hours (regular semester): Thursday 11am–4pm, Friday 11am–2pm, Saturday Closed

TACO BELL
Location: Old University Union (enter from main walkway, walk to back of building)
Description: Mexican-American fast food. Standard Taco Bell menu.
Hours (regular semester): Thursday 10:30am–7pm, Friday 10:30am–4pm, Saturday Closed

EINSTEIN BROS. BAGELS
Location: Standalone building between the Administration Office and the bottom floor of the Science Building
Description: High quality bagels and bagel sandwiches, sweet and savory options, pastries, and coffee drinks.
Hours (regular semester): Thursday 7:30am–6pm, Friday 7:30am–4pm, Saturday Closed

HALAL BITES OF CHICAGO
Location: New University Union (front of building, off the main walkway)
Description: On-campus location for a local food truck. Menu includes rice plates, gyros, falafel, and other halal options.
Hours (regular semester): Thursday 11am–4pm, Friday and Saturday Closed

STARBUCKS
Location: Old University Library, Bottom Floor (look for the Chase ATM as a landmark)
Description: Standard Starbucks menu — coffee drinks, teas, pastries, and sandwiches.
Hours (regular semester): Thursday 7:30am–7:30pm, Friday 7:30am–7:30pm, Saturday 10:30am–5pm

JAMBA JUICE
Location: Recreation and Wellness Center (RAW), to the right from the entrance
Description: Smoothies and juice drinks.
Hours (regular semester): Thursday and Friday 10am–4pm, Saturday Closed

MARKET
Location: Old University Union (enter through the front of the building)
Description: Campus convenience store. Sells a variety of snacks, drinks, frozen food, pre-made sandwiches and burritos, ramen, chips, and microwaveable items. Also carries packaged sushi rolls from Wild Blue.
Hours (regular semester): Thursday 8am–6pm, Friday 8am–4pm, Saturday Closed
